Overlay focus check

Click Reset and focus, keep typing in the box, then use the overlay shortcuts. The event log should stay empty and typing should continue. Switching to another app intentionally creates a log entry; reset after doing so.

  • Hide/show: Ctrl+Shift+H
  • Click-through: Ctrl+Shift+M (restore it after testing)
  • Move: Ctrl+Alt+arrows
  • Resize: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Up / Down
